## Editorial gates

Case-study fields exist in `content/case-studies.mjs`; all three supplied client references remain draft. Names alone do not establish scope, permission or results. A publishable record needs an approved summary, challenge, objective, service scope, execution, deliverables, media rights and client approval. Results are optional when unavailable; describe decisions and outputs honestly. Testimonials require exact approved wording and attribution.

`content/video-details.mjs` holds editorial metadata keyed by stable media IDs. The scanner records measured dimensions/duration separately. File modification time is not public upload date. Do not generate watch pages, VideoObject dates or a video sitemap from incomplete metadata. Add accessible captions/transcripts when speech or essential audio is present.

The four existing guide URLs now have bilingual buyer guidance. They are foundations for original examples, not claimed proprietary research. An industry hub, cost guide or comparison is released only when it contributes evidence beyond the existing service page.

## Measurement loop

After deployment and account verification, establish a 28-day baseline for landing pages, queries, device, language and actual enquiry submissions. Review indexing first; then compare qualified leads and query-to-page alignment over comparable periods. Track sales qualification outside analytics without sending names/emails into GA4. Separate branded from non-branded searches. Use change annotations; do not attribute every movement to this release. Improve pages that attract relevant searches but fail to explain fit or convert. Do not manufacture AI-visibility percentages from ordinary organic traffic.
