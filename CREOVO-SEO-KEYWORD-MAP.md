## Informational ownership and cannibalization rules

| Topic | English URL (Arabic mirrors /ar) | Intent | Service destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preparing a creative agency brief | /insights/creative-agency-brief/ | Process | Branding |
| Strategy versus identity | /insights/brand-strategy-vs-brand-identity/ | Comparison | Branding |
| Corporate video production planning | /insights/corporate-video-production-process/ | Process | Video production |
| Social management versus content creation | /insights/social-media-management-vs-content-creation/ | Comparison | Social media |

Service pages own commercial queries. Guides answer a distinct buying question and link to the service rather than duplicating its pitch. `/services/` is a directory; `/industries/` describes sectors welcomed, not proven sector expertise. No industry child pages or client case-study URLs are published without verified work, differentiated content and a clear conversion role. Cost questions stay within buyer guidance until Creovo can supply current scoped examples. No city pages, platform-specific pages or format-specific pages are generated merely from keyword variants.
