# Evidence-led content roadmap

| Priority | Topic/destination | Needed proof | Owner | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Existing Work → approved project study | Client permission, brief, delivered scope, assets, outcomes | Business owner + editor | Draft only; no fabricated results |
| P1 | Existing social management vs content guide | Current buyer objections and actual workflow | Service lead | Already published; monitor |
| P1 | Existing website redesign guide | Current migration checklist and ownership process | Technical lead | Already published; monitor |
| P2 | Video-production scope/pricing on existing service | Actual quoting variables, rights, revision process | Production lead | No invented packages or deadlines |
| P2 | Restaurant content strategy | Approved restaurant project and distinctive insights | Creative lead | Evidence gate before dedicated article |
| P2 | SEO vs Google Ads | Real decision framework and approved examples | Marketing lead | Separate informational intent; no duplicate agency landing page |
| P2 | Event coverage brief | Real permissions, shot list and delivery constraints | Production lead | One useful guide if enquiry data supports it |

Do not manufacture dates, experts, testimonials, awards, or keyword-count targets. Every new resource needs a primary service link and bilingual quality review.
