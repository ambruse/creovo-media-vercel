1 0 / H O W T O R E S E A R C H T H E S E V A R I A T I O N S
Validate language, then optimize intent.

Treat Qatar, Doha, locationless, shorthand, typo and Arabic queries as variations within service-intent clusters. Use real impressions
and conversion performance to decide what matters.

01           Start with Search Console, filtered to Qatar
            Open Performance, set the Qatar country filter, select Queries, export available rows and compare with unfiltered queries. Some
            uncommon or anonymized searches will not appear individually.

02          Label phrases by meaning and location
                  Classify each query as service, agency, industry or informational intent; then label country/city mention (yes/no), typo (yes/no),
                language, impressions, clicks and destination page.

03          Research the SERP from the target market
              Check whether Google shows agencies, freelancers, local listings, tutorials or job results. Keep a term only if its search intent matches
               a real Creovo offering.

04          Consolidate variants onto the best existing page
           Map "web designer doha," "website design" and "website making company" to one website-design intent cluster, rather than
                 publishing a page for each exact phrase.

05          Write clean copy and practical FAQs
             Use correct grammar and verified service descriptions. Answer customer questions naturally; add genuine case studies, deliverables
              and contact options. Keep the premium site design.

06         Review conversions, not only impressions
                Track qualified calls, forms, WhatsApp enquiries and lead value where measurable. Revisit query groupings monthly and refine service
               pages based on observed performance.



  Worked example: website design

   Primary page: /services/web-design/   | Core keyword: website design Qatar
   Variations to analyze: website design; web designer doha; website making company; make website for my business; webiste design; website designer
   near me.
   On-page execution: One natural title, one focused description, clear services, responsive design examples, relevant business FAQs and a direct
   proposal CTA - not a page per spelling.

1 2 / O N - S I T E E X E C U T I O N
Implementation plan for creovomedia.com

Maintain the premium, cinematic identity while helping search engines and potential clients understand precisely what Creovo offers
and why it is relevant.

01          Preserve all existing service URLs
                Assign a distinct primary keyword and a coherent related phrase set to each of the nine pages. Do not restructure URLs solely to
                  insert keywords.

02         Improve content clarity without redesigning
                Retain cinematic visuals and brand voice. Add descriptive service headings, crisp descriptions, useful process details, proof of work,
            CTA clarity and relevant FAQs.

03           Build selective industry-focused content
                  Start with restaurants, real estate and corporate/B2B clients if Creovo has authentic work or differentiated capability to show. Avoid
                 near-duplicate niche pages.

04          Strengthen portfolio and case studies
                 Explain the client context, the business problem, services delivered, creative output and measurable outcomes where available. Link
               examples back to their service pages.

05         Handle geographic relevance naturally

             make many interchangeable city pages.

06          Evaluate every decision with data
             Combine Search Console query/page exports with traffic, conversions and independent keyword research. Prioritize pages that can
                turn qualified organic visits into enquiries.
