## Primary implementation guidance

- [Google video best practices](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video): dedicated watch pages need one prominent playable video; a portfolio gallery is not automatically a video-indexable watch page. The current modal portfolio is not claimed as eligible.
- [Google localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions): reciprocal equivalent-language URLs and self-canonicals.
- [Google business representation](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177): public location/service-area eligibility must be checked before creating or changing a profile.
- [Google address management](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2853879): addresses are public; service-area businesses can hide them when customers are not received there.

Next research step after Search Console access: compare real query/page pairs, segment brand/nonbrand and language, identify overlap between service and guide URLs, then refine intent based on qualified enquiries. Search Console does not provide a universal reliable count of every AI answer citation; do not label ordinary organic traffic as measured AI visibility.
